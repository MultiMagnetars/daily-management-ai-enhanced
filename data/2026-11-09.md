TL;DR: 该文是一项以PRISMA 2020为指导的系统性文献综述，考察董事会（监事会）、审计委员会与内部审计在公司治理维护财务报告完整性中的作用；综合13项研究发现，审计委员会与财务报告质量的关联最为稳定，而独立董事（独立监事）的有效性取决于测量方式与监管情境，财务报告完整性代理指标的差异是研究结论不一致的主要来源。

Motivation: 研究问题：考察公司治理在维护财务报告完整性中的作用，重点聚焦董事会（监事会）、审计委员会与内部审计职能，并识别哪些治理机制与财务报告质量存在最稳定的关联、解释实证发现差异的成因。理论/研究背景：摘要指出，各研究在治理机制与财务报告完整性关系上存在不一致与情境差异，代理变量选择的差异被视为研究间不一致的主要来源之一。理论框架：摘要未说明。

Method: 数据与样本：采用四项检索查询共识别39条记录，其中13项研究符合纳入标准并纳入综合分析。核心变量/研究对象：董事会（监事会）、审计委员会（独立性、专业性、规模、会议频率）与内部审计职能，以及财务报告完整性的各类测量代理；并涉及印度尼西亚研究情境中的独立董事（独立监事）比例与最低监管门槛。研究方法：有限度的系统性文献综述，以PRISMA 2020框架为指导，采用叙事—主题分析方法识别重复出现的模式、情境差异与关系不一致之处，属于文献综述类研究。

Result: 主要结论：综合呈现三种主要模式。第一，审计委员会与财务报告质量的关联最为稳定，尤其通过其独立性、专业性、规模与会议频率体现。第二，独立董事（独立监事）的有效性取决于测量方式与监管情境；在印度尼西亚研究中，当其比例仅达到最低监管门槛时，效应往往在统计上不显著。第三，当专业胜任能力与独立性同时具备时，内部审计对财务报告监督的贡献更有效，且风险监督委员会的参与可进一步增强其有效性。进一步结果：用于衡量财务报告完整性的代理变量差异是研究间不一致的主要来源。作用机制：摘要未说明。调节效应：摘要未说明。异质性：摘要仅以描述性方式指出不同测量方式、监管与制度情境下的差异，未报告分组检验。

Conclusion: 理论/文献贡献：提出一个整合董事会（监事会）、审计委员会与内部审计职能的三层财务报告监督概念框架，其贡献在于解释这些机制如何相互作用，以及其有效性为何会因制度情境与测量情境而异。实践/政策启示：研究结果凸显强化实质性独立性、专业专长以及监督机制之间协调的必要性，而非仅依赖对监管要求的形式合规；未来研究应开发更为一致的财务报告完整性测量指标，并在不同行业与监管环境中对所提出的框架进行实证检验。

Abstract: Abstract Objective: This study aims to examine the role of corporate governance in safeguarding financial reporting integrity, with particular emphasis on the board of commissioners, audit committee, and internal audit function. It seeks to identify the governance mechanisms most consistently associated with financial reporting quality and explain the factors underlying variations in empirical findings. Method: This study employs a limited systematic literature review guided by the PRISMA 2020 framework. A total of 39 records were identified through four search queries, of which 13 studies met the eligibility criteria and were included in the synthesis. The selected studies were analyzed using a narrative-thematic approach to identify recurring patterns, contextual differences, and inconsistencies in the relationships between corporate governance mechanisms and financial reporting integrity. Results: The synthesis reveals three main patterns. First, the audit committee demonstrates the most consistent association with financial reporting quality, particularly through its independence, expertise, size, and meeting frequency. Second, the effectiveness of independent commissioners is contingent on measurement approaches and regulatory contexts. In Indonesian studies, their effect is often statistically insignificant when their proportion merely meets the minimum regulatory threshold. Third, internal audit contributes more effectively to financial reporting oversight when professional competence and independence are present simultaneously, with its effectiveness further strengthened by the involvement of risk oversight committees. Differences in the proxies used to measure financial reporting integrity emerge as a major source of inconsistency across studies. Conclusion/Novelty/Implications: This study proposes a three-layer conceptual framework of financial reporting oversight integrating the board of commissioners, audit committee, and internal audit function. Its contribution lies in explaining how these mechanisms interact and why their effectiveness varies across institutional and measurement contexts. The findings highlight the need to strengthen substantive independence, professional expertise, and coordination among oversight mechanisms rather than relying solely on formal compliance with regulatory requirements. Future research should develop more consistent measures of financial reporting integrity and empirically test the proposed framework across different industries and regulatory settings.
