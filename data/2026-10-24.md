Motivation: 研究问题：在发展经济体面临持续制度约束的背景下，商业模式创新（BMI）如何以及在何种条件下促进中小企业的可持续绩效。理论/研究背景：中小企业可持续绩效的追求在发展经济体中日益重要，而关于BMI与可持续绩效关系的既有证据被认为是碎片化且不一致的，本研究据此进行综合梳理。理论框架：摘要明确援引动态能力理论与利益相关者理论。

Method: 数据与样本：通过Scopus、Web of Science和Google Scholar检索2014年至2026年发表的同行评议文章，最终纳入37项实证研究。核心变量/研究对象：BMI与中小企业可持续绩效的关系，以及绿色与生态创新、数字化转型、融资可得性、制度支持、技术能力等主题要素。研究方法：采用系统综述与元分析优先报告条目（PRISMA）框架进行系统综述，并对纳入文献开展主题综合（thematic synthesis）；属于系统性文献综述类研究。

Result: 主要结论：综合识别出五个主导主题——BMI作为可持续绩效的驱动因素、可持续绩效作为多维构念、绿色与生态创新的中介作用、数字化转型作为BMI的促进因素、以及向整合的可持续导向商业模式的转型；BMI通过重构价值创造、价值传递与价值获取来提升财务、环境与社会绩效，但该关系具有情境依赖性，并取决于内部动态能力与外部利益相关者期望的一致性。作用机制：数字化转型与绿色创新是摘要明确指出的关键中介机制（绿色与生态创新的中介作用、数字化转型作为促进因素）。调节效应：融资可得性、制度支持与技术能力被表述为显著的背景性调节因素。异质性/情境限制：所综述证据在地理上集中于尼日利亚，可推广性仍然有限。经济后果/进一步结果：摘要未说明。需注意：上述结论为文献综合层面的一致性与条件性判断，摘要未报告具体的效应量或统计显著性数值。

Conclusion: 理论/文献贡献：提供了一项整合的、以理论（动态能力理论与利益相关者理论）为基础的综合，勾勒出BMI推动可持续绩效的情境特定路径，并整合了既有碎片化且不一致的实证证据。实践/政策启示：摘要指出该综述对发展经济体中的管理者和政策制定者具有实践启示，具体启示内容摘要未说明。

Abstract: The pursuit of sustainable performance among small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) has become increasingly important in developing economies that face persistent institutional constraints, yet existing evidence on the relationship between business model innovation (BMI) and sustainable performance remains fragmented and inconsistent. Drawing on Dynamic Capabilities Theory and Stakeholder Theory, this study synthesises the empirical literature to clarify how, and under what conditions, BMI contributes to the sustainable performance of SMEs. Adopting the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) framework, peer-reviewed articles published between 2014 and 2026 were identified from Scopus, Web of Science, and Google Scholar, yielding 37 empirical studies for thematic synthesis. The synthesis identified five dominant themes: BMI as a driver of sustainable performance; sustainable performance as a multidimensional construct; the mediating role of green and eco-innovation; digital transformation as a facilitator of BMI; and the transition toward integrated, sustainability-focused business models. Digital transformation and green innovation emerged as key mediating mechanisms, while access to finance, institutional support, and technological capability operated as significant contextual moderators. The findings indicate that BMI enhances financial, environmental, and social performance by reconfiguring value creation, delivery, and capture, but that this relationship is context-dependent and contingent on the alignment between internal dynamic capabilities and external stakeholder expectations. Because the reviewed evidence is geographically concentrated in Nigeria, generalisability remains limited. The review contributes an integrated, theory-grounded synthesis that maps context-specific pathways for sustainable BMI adoption, with practical implications for managers and policymakers in developing economies.
