Main category: management

TL;DR: 本研究考察孟加拉国上市银行业中CEO自恋与环境报告之间的关联，使用2018—2024年245个公司—年度观测值，以年度报告中CEO照片突出程度和签名尺寸衡量CEO自恋。结果显示，与高层梯队理论一致，CEO自恋与环境报告程度呈正向且统计显著的关联，且该关联在较大企业中更强，并受CEO薪酬正向调节；但这种披露增加并不必然反映实质性环境绩效。

Motivation: 研究问题：CEO自恋是否以及如何与孟加拉国银行业企业的环境报告程度相关联。理论/研究背景：摘要指出研究以高层梯队理论（upper echelons theory）为解释基础，关注高管人格特质对披露行为的影响，并强调该研究提供来自新兴经济体、关于CEO自恋如何塑造环境报告行为的最初证据；摘要未具体说明此前文献存在的缺口。理论框架：高层梯队理论（摘要明确提及）。

Method: 数据与样本：孟加拉国银行业，达卡证券交易所上市银行，245个公司—年度观测值，样本期为2018—2024年。核心变量/研究对象：解释变量为CEO自恋，采用两个指标衡量——（1）年度报告中CEO照片的突出程度；（2）CEO签名的尺寸；被解释变量为环境报告程度；进一步分析涉及企业规模与CEO薪酬。研究方法：实证面板分析，摘要明确采用静态面板估计与动态面板估计来检验假设。数据库、抽样细节与控制变量：摘要未说明。

Conclusion: 理论/文献贡献：摘要指出本研究提供了来自新兴经济体关于CEO自恋如何塑造环境报告行为的首个证据，并通过区分披露程度与实质性承诺，对高管人格、自愿披露与企业可持续性文献作出贡献。实践/政策启示：董事会、监管者与政策制定者需批判性评估环境披露的可信度，尤其在报告在很大程度上属自愿性的情境中；强化治理机制、提高披露标准与引入独立鉴证可能有助于缓解自恋型领导下的象征性报告与漂绿风险。

Abstract: Purpose The study aims to investigate the association between chief executive officer (CEO) narcissism and environmental reporting in the banking industry of Bangladesh. Design/methodology/approach This study employs a sample of 245 firm-year observations from the listed banks of Dhaka Stock Exchange spanning the period during 2018–2024. To measure CEO narcissism, we used two distinct indicators: (1) the prominence of the CEO's photograph in the annual report and (2) the size of the CEO's signature. The analysis employs both static and dynamic panel estimations to test the hypotheses. Findings Consistent with upper echelons theory, the results reveal a positive and statistically significant association between CEO narcissism and the extent of environmental reporting. Further analyses indicate that the association is stronger in larger firms and is positively moderated by CEO compensation, suggesting that organizational visibility and financial incentives amplify the disclosure-enhancing effects of narcissistic leadership. Importantly, the findings indicate an increase in the extent of environmental disclosures, rather than necessarily reflecting substantive environmental performance. Practical implications The results highlight the need for boards, regulators and policymakers to critically assess the credibility of environmental disclosures, particularly in contexts where reporting is largely voluntary. Strengthened governance mechanisms, enhanced disclosure standards and independent assurance may help mitigate the risk of symbolic reporting and greenwashing under narcissistic leadership. Originality/value This study provides the first evidence from an emerging economy on how CEO narcissism shapes environmental reporting behavior. By distinguishing between disclosure extent and substantive commitment, it contributes to the literature on executive personality, voluntary disclosure and corporate sustainability in emerging-market contexts.
