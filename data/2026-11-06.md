TL;DR: 本研究以中国30个省级区域2013—2024年平衡面板数据，考察数字经济发展与农业绿色发展（AGD）之间的关系及空间溢出效应。结果显示数字经济发展与AGD显著改善相关，农业技术创新占估计效应的12.67%，农村人力资本存在门槛效应，且估计的省际溢出效应大于省内直接效应。

Method: 数据与样本：中国30个省级区域2013—2024年的平衡面板数据；数据库来源摘要未说明。核心变量/研究对象：数字经济多维指数与农业绿色发展（AGD）多维指数，均以熵值法计算；农业技术创新作为中介变量，农村人力资本作为门槛变量。研究方法：实证面板分析，采用省份与年份固定效应、中介框架、门槛估计以及空间杜宾设定（spatial Durbin specification），并进行分区域估计与空间分解。

Abstract: The transition toward greener agricultural production increasingly depends on the effective integration of digital technologies into rural production and service systems. Using a balanced panel covering 30 provincial-level regions in China from 2013 to 2024, this study evaluates the relationship between digital-economy development and agricultural green development (AGD). The two multidimensional indices are calculated through the entropy-weighting method. The empirical analysis proceeds through province and year fixed effects, a mediation framework, threshold estimation, and a spatial Durbin specification. The results show that higher digital-economy development is associated with a significant improvement in AGD. Agricultural technology innovation constitutes a transmission channel and accounts for 12.67% of the estimated effect. The relationship is also conditional on rural human capital: after rural human capital crosses the estimated threshold, the positive association becomes noticeably stronger. Regional estimates reveal substantial heterogeneity, with the eastern provinces exhibiting a larger effect than the central and western regions. Spatial decomposition further indicates that the influence of digital development extends beyond provincial boundaries, and the estimated spillover effect is greater than the within-province direct effect. Taken together, the findings suggest that digital transformation in agriculture depends not only on technological infrastructure but also on local absorptive capacity and cross-regional linkages. The results therefore highlight the importance of strengthening rural human capital, agricultural technology diffusion, and interregional cooperation in the process of agricultural green transformation.
