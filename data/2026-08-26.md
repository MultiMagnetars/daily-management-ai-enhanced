Abstract: This study examined the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) strategies adopted by Banks to enhance and develop their immediate environments in Lagos State, Nigeria. This study was motivated by the increasing demand for banks to be active in sustainable community development through socially responsible programs. The study specifically analysed the CSR activities undertaken by banks, evaluated the strategies they have used to implement them, and identified the extent to which they have contributed to environmental and community development. Stakeholder Theory and Social Exchange Theory served as the basis for the study. The descriptive survey research design was used. The population under this study was the estimated population of Lagos State, which is 12,791,699 persons. The number of respondents was calculated at the 5% level of significance using Taro Yamane's formula, yielding 400. The data were gathered using a structured questionnaire and analysed using descriptive statistics, including frequency counts and percentages. The results showed that the banks are actively involved in various CSR activities, such as educational support programmes, healthcare interventions, environmental sustainability projects and community development programmes. It also showed that stakeholder consultations, community engagement, and strategic partnerships are key strategies for implementing CSR programmes. A majority of respondents also felt that the bank's CSRs' activities were beneficial for the development of their communities and the environment in their localities. The study concluded that CSR is now seen as an important instrument for encouraging sustainable development and improving the bonds between banks and host communities. On the community level, the study suggested greater investment in community-based CSR projects, greater community involvement, regular evaluation of the impact of CSR programmes and a greater focus on environmental sustainability projects to maximise the development outcomes of CSR programmes.
